[H2] Industrial Visit Program
Students gain real-world exposure beyond the classroom through immersive industrial visits that provide firsthand insight into corporate environments, workplace culture, and operational workflows. These visits allow learners to observe how organizations function on a daily basis, interact with industry professionals, and understand real job roles, responsibilities, and expectations.
By connecting theoretical knowledge with practical applications, students develop a clearer understanding of how their academic learning translates into real industry scenarios. This exposure helps them build confidence, gain career clarity, and make informed decisions about their professional paths. Industrial visits also inspire students by showcasing diverse career opportunities and preparing them to transition smoothly from education to employment.
