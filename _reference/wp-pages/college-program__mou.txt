[H2] What You Will Explore in This Program
We establish Memorandums of Understanding with educational institutions to promote long-term collaboration and continuous student and faculty development. Through MoU partnerships, institutions receive access to free or sponsored internships for students, priority workshops and Value Added Courses, joint certification programs, and ongoing industry mentorship support. These collaborations aim to strengthen industry–institute relationships, provide consistent skill development opportunities, and support institutional growth through practical industry engagement.
